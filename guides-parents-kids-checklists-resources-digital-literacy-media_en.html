FAQ: Frequently Asked Questions

Why do families need the Cybear app? expand_more

Prepping kids to safely navigate a complex world of algorithms, cyberbullying, misinformation, and high-trigger habits is a massive undertaking. The app takes the weight off your shoulders by teaching kids the core safety foundations in a way that sticks. They can learn at their own pace, and because modules can be repeated anytime, you don't have to keep playing the repeating record.

How much does Cybear cost? expand_more

The app is expected to launch between €4 and €9, though we haven't locked down final pricing just yet while final development is wrapping up.

How does the app actually work? expand_more

Kids are guided through digital literacy and safety milestones using age-appropriate, gamified challenges, quick quizzes, scenario comparisons, and behavioral tests. Parents get a clear dashboard view to track what their kids have mastered and see where they might still need a little extra support.
